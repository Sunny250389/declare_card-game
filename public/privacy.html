Privacy Policy

Declare card game. Last updated: 30 October 2026.

This policy explains what information the Declare app and its online service collect, why, and how you can ask us to delete it. The app is developed by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]. Contact: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a username. If you sign up, we store your password only as a salted hash, never in readable form.
  • Verification and reset codes: six-digit codes sent to your email. They are stored as hashes and expire after 15 minutes.
  • Sessions: a random sign-in token, stored as a hash on our server and kept on your device so you stay signed in. Sessions last up to 30 days.
  • Online game data: the rooms you create or join, your player name, your cards and moves during an online match, and match scores.
  • Security records: the number of failed sign-in attempts per email, used to lock accounts temporarily against guessing.
  • On-device data: your settings, the server address you entered, your sign-in token, and any unfinished local match. This is stored only on your device.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, payment information, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.

2. How we use information

  • To create and sign in to your account.
  • To send verification and password-reset codes to your email address.
  • To run online rooms and matches, and show each player only their own cards.
  • To protect accounts from abuse, such as repeated sign-in or code guessing.

3. Email

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the service providers needed to run Declare: the hosting provider for our server ([NAME OF HOSTING PROVIDER]) and the email provider named above. Other players in an online room can see your username and your match actions. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. How long we keep information

  • Account details are kept until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Verification codes expire after 15 minutes. Sessions expire after 30 days.
  • Room and match data is kept while the room exists. A room is removed when its last player leaves.
  • On-device data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

6. Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Sign-in tokens and codes are stored as hashes. Connections to our server use HTTPS. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password for Declare.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

You can sign out at any time, which removes the sign-in token from your device. You can clear an unfinished local match from the app. To delete your account and the data linked to it, email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you signed up with. We will delete your account and its data within 30 days.

8. Children

Declare is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
